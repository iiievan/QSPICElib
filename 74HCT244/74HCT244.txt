* ============================================================================
* 74HCT244 - vosmerichnyj bufer s tretjim sostojaniem, povedencheskaja model'
*
* Istochnik: onsemi MM74HCT240/MM74HCT244, Publication Order MM74HCT244/D,
*            Rev.3, June 2024
* Versija modeli: 1.1
* Kalibrovka:    ngspice-42 + QSpice, .tran s max shagom 0.05n
*
* ISTORIJA
*   1.0 - bazovaja versija, 3 stupeni, test A5 ne prohodil
*   1.1 - dobavlena zaderzhka razreshenija vyhoda (TPDZ), A5 prohodit;
*         TTOL vynesen v params; dobavlena tablica temperaturnyh uglov
*
* ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
* STRUKTURA - TRI STUPENI, i eto prinicipial'no:
*   1. vhodnoj porog VTH (S_I1/S_I2) - bez zaderzhki
*   2. RC-zaderzhka s porogami na SEREDINE razmaha (2.5 V +- VDB)
*   3. vyhodnoj totem-pole ROH / ROL + kljuch tretjego sostojanija
* Odnostupenchataja versija (RC srazu ot vhoda s porogom 1.4 V) dajot
* nesimmetrichnye zaderzhki: tPLH 8.8 ns protiv tPHL 24.9 ns pri
* dashitnyh 14 ns, potomu chto 1.4 V ne na seredine razmaha 0..5 V.
* Cep' razreshenija OEB postroena tak zhe, po toj zhe prichine.
*
* ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
* TEMPERATURNYE UGLY
* VNIMANIE: model' NE imeet temperaturnoj zavisimosti. .step temp na nejo
* ne dejstvuet voobshche. Ugly zadajutsja NABOROM PARAMETROV - tak zhe,
* kak dashit zadajot ih otdel'nymi kolonkami garantirovannyh predelov.
*
*  CORNER  uslovie dashita        ROH    ROL    TPD     TPDZ
*  --------------------------------------------------------
*    1     +25 C, tipovye          50    33.3    9n     14n
*    2     +25 C, predel          86.7   43.3   12.2n   19n
*    3     -40...+85 C, predel   110     55     15n     23n
*    4     -55...+125 C, predel  133     66.7   18n     28n
*
* ROH i ROL vychisleny iz DC-tablicy pri VCC=4.5 V, |IOUT|=6 mA:
*   ROH = (4.5 - VOH)/6mA ,  ROL = VOL/6mA
* TPD i TPDZ podobrany po AC-tablice tak, chtoby izmerennye tPLH i tPZH
* legli na sootvetstvujushchij predel. Sm. fajl tests/74HCT244_test.cir
*
* ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
* PARAMETRY
*   ROH   vyhodnoe soprotivlenie pri vysokom urovne, Om
*   ROL   to zhe pri nizkom, Om
*   TPD   zaderzhka rasprostranenija vhod-vyhod (podgonochnaja)
*   TPDZ  zaderzhka razreshenija/zapreta vyhoda (podgonochnaja)
*   VTH   vhodnoj porog. 1.4 V - seredina mezhdu VIL=0.8 i VIH=2.0 dashita.
*         Imenno poetomu 3.3 V s nozhki MK chitaetsja kak logicheskaja "1"
*   VHY   gisterezis kljuchej
*   VDB   raznos porogov verhnego i nizhnego kljucha (break-before-make)
*   CIN   vhodnaja jomkost' (dashit: max 15 pF)
*   COUT  vyhodnaja jomkost' (dashit: typ 15 pF, max 20 pF)
*   TTOL  vremennoe razreshenie vokrug sobytija pereklyuchenija (TOL'KO QSpice)
*
* ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
* PRO TTOL
* QSpice schitaet s adaptivnym shagom. Kljuchi S - razryvnye elementy:
* sam moment pereklyuchenija naxoditsja tochno, no SLEDUJUSHCHAJA tochka
* mozhet okazat'sja daleko, i .meas nachinaet interpolirovat' mimo.
* Proverено eksperimentom na etoj modeli (.tran bez ogranichenija shaga):
*   TTOL=1n   -> tPLH = 14.94 ns
*   TTOL=200n -> tPLH = 16.97 ns   (+14 %, front pri etom vygljadit normal'no)
* To est' degradacija ne lomaet kartinku, a TIHO smeshchaet rezul'tat -
* eto opasnee, chem javnaja lomanaja.
* Vazhno: fronty (tTLH/tTHL) ot TTOL prakticheski ne zavisjat - oni sami
* zastavljajut reshatel' mel'chit'. Stradajut tol'ko izmerenija po ODNOMU
* peresecheniju urovnja, to est' zaderzhki.
*
* ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
* CHEGO MODEL' NE VOSPROIZVODIT (proverjat' po dashitu glazami)
*   - IOZ (utechka v Hi-Z) max 2.5 mkA pri +85 C, 10 mkA pri +125 C
*   - IOUT abs max +-35 mA - eto DC-rejting, ne impul'snyj
*   - ICC, CPD (90 pF na bufer v aktivnom sostojanii)
*   - zavisimost' ot VCC vne diapazona 4.5...5.5 V
*   - diapazon MM74HCT244: -55...+125 C
* ============================================================================
.subckt 74HCT244 IN OEB OUT VCC GND
+ params: ROH=50 ROL=33.3 TPD=9n TPDZ=14n VTH=1.4 VHY=0.2 VDB=0.25
+ CIN=15p COUT=15p TTOL=1n
* --- stupen' 1: vhodnoj porog ---
C_IN  IN   GND {CIN}
S_I1  VCC  nlog IN  GND SWIN  TTOL={TTOL}
S_I2  nlog GND  GND IN  SWINB TTOL={TTOL}
R_LG  nlog GND  1meg
* --- stupen' 2: zaderzhka signal'nogo trakta ---
R_DEL nlog nd   1k
C_DEL nd   GND  {1.4427*TPD/1k}
* --- cep' razreshenija: porog + zaderzhka ---
S_Z1  VCC  noeb OEB GND SWIN  TTOL={TTOL}
S_Z2  noeb GND  GND OEB SWINB TTOL={TTOL}
R_LZ  noeb GND  1meg
R_ZD  noeb nz   1k
C_ZD  nz   GND  {1.4427*TPDZ/1k}
* --- stupen' 3: vyhodnoj kaskad ---
S_UP  VCC  nmid nd  GND SWUP  TTOL={TTOL}
S_DN  nmid GND  GND nd  SWDN  TTOL={TTOL}
S_OE  nmid OUT  GND nz  SWOE  TTOL={TTOL}
C_OUT OUT  GND  {COUT}
R_LK  nmid GND  100meg
.model SWIN  SW Ron=100    Roff=1g Vt={VTH}        Vh=0.05
.model SWINB SW Ron=100    Roff=1g Vt={-VTH}       Vh=0.05
.model SWUP  SW Ron={ROH}  Roff=1g Vt={2.5+VDB}    Vh={VHY}
.model SWDN  SW Ron={ROL}  Roff=1g Vt={-(2.5-VDB)} Vh={VHY}
.model SWOE  SW Ron=1m     Roff=1g Vt=-2.5         Vh={VHY}
.ends 74HCT244
