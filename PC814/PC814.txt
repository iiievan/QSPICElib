* PC814 v0.2 - 25 C behavioral model of an AC-input optocoupler.
* Supplied Sharp PC814 Series data sheet (pages 2-4); compare likely
* Lite-On LTV-814S package/temperature separately before procurement.
* Pins: LED 1, LED 2, collector 4, emitter 3. Example:
* X1 led1 led2 collector emitter PC814 params: CTR1M=0.5
* CTR1M = IC/IF at IF=1mA and VCE=5V, at 25 C (dimensionless).
* Rank A: 0.5 .. 1.5; unselected: 0.2 .. 3.0. Default 0.5 matches the
* representative Fig. 5 curve at 1mA, not a guaranteed typical value.
* Fig. 5 shape was fitted only for IF about 0.5 .. 20mA. Outside this
* interval, neither the curve nor output absolute maxima are guaranteed.
* TAU=2u gives an approximate 4.4us 10%-90% optical step response.
* Only the diode's intrinsic SPICE temperature scaling is present; CTR,
* switching/storage and leakage have no validated temperature dependence.
